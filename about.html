About Me

My Journey

My journey into web development began with curiosity about how the internet really works. While pursuing my First Year in Pre-Engineering at PECHS College, I wanted to do something creative and technical at the same time — that’s when I discovered my passion for coding. To turn that passion into skill, I joined SMIT (Saylani Mass IT Training Program), where I’m learning HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, along with backend fundamentals. Each day, I’m growing as a developer — building projects, improving my logic, and shaping my path toward becoming a full-stack web developer.

⚡ What I Enjoy Building

I love bringing ideas to life through clean, responsive, and fast front-end experiences. I enjoy creating interactive designs that not only look good but also feel smooth to use. On the backend side, I’m learning to build simple, secure, and maintainable APIs that connect everything together. I also enjoy working on reusable components and mini design systems that make projects more scalable and consistent.
